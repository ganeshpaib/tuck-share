What TuckMe is

TuckMe lets you save links, places, pictures and notes from other apps, sort them into collections, find them on a map and share them as links. It's free. There's no account, and what you save is kept on your phone.

Sharing links

Places, maps and distances

TuckMe finds a place's location automatically from what you saved, using map services such as OpenStreetMap and Photon. Pins can be wrong, out of date or missing, and distances are rough straight-line estimates. Opening hours, closures and whether a place still exists aren't checked. Please check the location in a maps app before you travel. Directions are provided by Apple Maps or Google Maps, not TuckMe.
