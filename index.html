Ways to find it again

Every place, on one map

Saves with a location get a pin. Map links find their own, and you can add a place to anything else. Filter by category or #tag, then tap Open in Maps.

Recipes, tagged for you

Make a Recipes category and recipe links go straight into it, tagged veg, quick or dessert. Add your own #tags too, then filter with a tap.

Search what you remember

Type any word you remember from a save: its title, caption, note, place or #tag.

A nudge on Sunday

Every Sunday at 10, a reminder of saves you haven't opened in a while. When there's nothing to say, there's no notification.

Categories you make

Date night, Gifts, Trips: make one for anything, each in its own colour. Swipe a save right to move it somewhere else.
